the poem i didn't know i was writing

I’ve never been stable in this life, nor am I sure I ever will,
The thought of someone loving me feels so surreal.
The doubts inside my mind are loud,
What if you wake up and step out of our cloud?

The cloud we built just for us,
Our little world built purely on trust.
I’ve never let someone in this deep,
Maybe I just like having a promise to keep.

I want to be seen only by you,
Where the truest parts of me come into view.
The expectations of others make me drown,
But with you, my heart finally settles down.

It’s easy in a way that feels natural,
I can’t believe I ever thought this was casual.
I’ve never smiled and cried so much with one person,
I guess I found my only exception.

The smile I get at the mere thought of you,
And the tears that form because I know I love you.
Loving someone this deeply makes me feel uneasy,
A gentle fear that washes over me completely.

Uneasy because I know I’ll never let go,
Why would I ever choose to leave my home?
I guess it’s true when you love someone, you finally see:
A brand new version of who you can be.

Someone who can be no one and everything all at once,
Without the constant fear that you'll leave when we're done.
Commitment used to terrify me,
Given all the history I have with everybody.

But with you, I know that I’m ready,
I’ve already given you my heart, safe and steady.
Call it cheesy, but I still want to ask properly:
In this crazy life, will you stand by side?

In my unstable days, will you still put up a fight?
On the days I forget who I am,
Will you hold my hand and help me stand?
All these questions lead to the very end:

Will you do me the honor of becoming my girlfriend?

Adi, will you be my girlfriend?

She said yes.
